About SANS2D Beamline

ISIS Neutron and Muon Source (TS2)

SANS2D is a world-class time-of-flight Small-Angle Neutron Scattering diffractometer located at the Second Target Station (TS2) of the ISIS Neutron and Muon Source in the United Kingdom.

By utilizing a broad range of neutron wavelengths simultaneously through time-of-flight techniques, SANS2D covers an exceptionally wide momentum transfer range ($Q$-range) in a single measurement. This makes it ideal for studying complex structures in soft matter, polymers, biological macromolecules, colloids, and advanced materials on length scales from 1 to 500 nm.

Key Instrument Highlights
  • Moderator: E2 viewing coupled cold hydrogen moderator at ~20K.
  • Flight Paths: Flexible primary collimation up to 12m and a 12m long secondary vacuum tank.
  • Detectors: Two movable $1\text{m}^2$ ${}^3\text{He-CF}_4$ ORDELA area detectors offering versatile scattering angle coverage.
